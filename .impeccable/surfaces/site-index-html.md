# Cipher League app

Mode: Operate. Audience: students in a cyber club, often on phones. Job: know what to do next, draft, set a lineup, follow a matchup and check where points came from. Guests browse players, rules and sources before joining. Chosen look: Line Map (user picked it on the decision page).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Seven positions are seven lines on one map; the snake draft is a route you ride. Refuses the dark hacker dashboard with a sidebar and stat cards.

OWN-WORLD: White station-wall ground, one black sign band as the shell, seven saturated line colours each tied to a fixed letter in a round bullet (F S D V T L A, bench B). Overpass throughout. Square-ish signs, heavy rules, no shadows, gradients or glyph icons. Pending is a dashed line, never only a colour.

STORY: See the next action, understand the position, make the pick or lineup, open the evidence behind a score.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black header with brand and four destinations; one headline naming the next action and its single primary button; below it the line diagram or route for the current state; then the list that action works on.

FORM: transit wayfinding system; position 5 of the ordered list; seed:377db5e5cb1f4980. Raises: pending/provisional/final differ by line style; one fixed letter per position; lineup fits one phone screen; matchups compare seven positions side by side; every score sits beside its evidence link.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
